Task mapping interview

A set of instructions for an AI assistant. It interviews you about your work, a sentence or two at a time, finds a task an agent could help with, maps it out, and builds a page of ideas with working mockups.

Keep your data safe. Don't paste confidential, client or personal information into an AI tool unless your organisation allows it. The interview only needs your work described in general terms. Connecting an AI tool to work systems such as email or calendars may need your IT department's permission.

Four ways to use it

Claude and Claude Cowork

Add it as a skill

  1. Download the skill (.zip).
  2. In Claude, add it to your skills (Anthropic's guide: Using skills in Claude, or ask Claude how). Skills you add work in both Claude chat and Cowork.
  3. Start a new chat and type:

/task-mapping-interview

Your organisation may need to allow skills before you can add one.
