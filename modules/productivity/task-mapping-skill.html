Task mapping interview

A prompt to paste into an AI assistant. It interviews you about your work, a sentence or two at a time, finds a task an agent could help with, maps it out, and builds a page of ideas with working mockups.

Keep your data safe. Don't paste confidential, client or personal information into an AI tool unless your organisation allows it. The interview only needs your work described in general terms. Connecting an AI tool to work systems such as email or calendars may need your IT department's permission.

How to use it

  1. Copy the prompt below.
  2. Open a new chat in the AI assistant you use: Claude, Cowork, ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini or another.
  3. Paste it in and send it.

The assistant will start the interview straight away. If it can't make pages with working mockups, it will write the ideas out as a document instead.

The prompt

You don't need to read it to use it. It's here if you're curious what you're asking the assistant to do.
